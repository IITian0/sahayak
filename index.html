How it works

Four steps, every single day

The caregiver stops firefighting. She knows what to do, has someone to ask, and can finally rest — while the family stays in the loop.

Onboard

One short call sets up the elder's profile, medicines and routine.

Guide

Every morning, a voice checklist in the caregiver's own language.

Respite

A verified helper is matched and booked for a few hours.

Update

The family living away gets a short end-of-day note.

What you get

Built for the caregiver, not the patient

Most tools centre the person being cared for. Sahayak centres the person doing the caring — because that is where the load actually falls.

◍

Daily care guide

A voice checklist in Hindi or Marathi: medicines, meals, what to watch for. No English forms.

◌

Caregiver circle

Ask other families who are living the same day. Real answers, not search results.

✚

Verified respite

Book a trained, background-checked helper for a few hours. Every visit is tracked.

⌂

Family updates

Sons and daughters away from home get a short daily note — and stop worrying.

✎

Clinical review

A doctor and a nurse review every care step before it ships.

₹

Affordable

₹199–499 a month, against ₹15,000–75,000 for urban home care today.

For hospitals & employers

Care that continues after discharge

Most families fall apart in the week after a patient comes home. We sell a care-at-home pack alongside discharge, and a plan employers can give staff who look after parents.

✚

Hospital discharge pack

A structured 30-day care plan, a trained helper and daily updates — sold with the discharge, billed per patient.

◫

Corporate elder benefit

Employers pay per employee, per year. Staff caring for parents take fewer unplanned days off.
